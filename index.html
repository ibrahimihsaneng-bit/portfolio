Frequently Asked Questions

Methodologies & Working Protocols

Common technical and engagement inquiries answered for academic researchers and engineering clients.

Numerical CFD and FEA models are always correlated against experimental wind tunnel data (such as Abbott & von Doenhoff for airfoils), published analytical formulations (Euler-Bernoulli beam theory, 1D fin networks), or recognized industry standards (ISO 15614-1 for weld cooling rates). Mesh independence checks and residual convergence thresholds (< 10⁻⁵ to 10⁻⁶) are documented for every deliverable.
Initial geometry meshing and preliminary convergence runs are typically completed within 3 business days. Full multi-case parametric sweeps, post-processed streamline/stress contours, and final engineering dossiers are delivered within 5 to 7 days depending on domain complexity.
